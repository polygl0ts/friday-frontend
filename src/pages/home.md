# Welcome to polylg0ts

You're looking at the polygl0ts friday platform, our favorite one-day-a-week platform
that runs throughout the whole week.

Never played CTFs before? Don't worry! We're active on the [polygl0ts discord](https://discord.gg/7Zx4FZyTSP) and are happy to answer any questions. We hold meetings every friday at 17h at EPFL, come hang out, ask questions, play CTFs, and eat pizza!

First you'll need to [login](https://friday.polygl0ts.ch/login). We'll send you a login token, save that where you would normally save your passwords. Then you can check out our [Friday Challenges](https://friday.polygl0ts.ch/chall). They are meant to ease you into CTFs and are for beginner and intermediate players alike.

## Rules

+ **No AI**. AI has been trained on the blood sweat & tears of many a CTF writeup and is currently on par with an expert player. All[^1] CTFs that we play as [polygl0ts](https://polygl0ts.ch/) and 0rganizers we will play in the human-only category. Watching a prompt go down across your screen is not fun. If you want to get a job in pentesting you will certainly use AI there, but you will only be able to differentiate yourself with the knowledge you gained by playing without it.
+ **You may attack the infra**. No DoS, but like if you find a bug in the infra let us know.
+ **No flag-sharing**. If you need help, feel free to ask a friend (or us) to explain you something, but never share flags.
+ **English comms**. The friday meetings and discord channels are english-first.
+ **Be nice**. Don't discriminate etc. etc. religion, queerness, etc. etc.

[^1]: We may make exception's from time to time, but generally noone wants to play "open division" CTFs.
